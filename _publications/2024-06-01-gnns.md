Long before Semantic IDs, during my internship at Yandex, I was conducting research on the optimal ways to represent item sequences in transformer-based recommender models. Since Yandex is a large industry, my main focus was on industrial solutions, such as multihash ID embeddings (used by Google), and GNN item embeddings (used by Pinterest). First of all, I tried these solutions on Yandex's largest platforms: Yandex Market and Yandex Music, where training data can be considered nearly unlimited. Although pretrained GNN item embeddings improved recommender's metrics, we did not consider an additional pretraining stage worthwhile for production. Out of curiosity, I also compared these two strategies on a smaller Yandex platform and on a publicly available dataset. Here, the conclusion was opposite: when the training data is notably limited, pretraining item embeddings benefits significantly.
